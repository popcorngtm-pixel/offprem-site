OffPrem/About

About

A trade press built for the industry running off the menu.

OffPrem covers restaurant technology news, deals, economics, and marketing — written for the people building the tools and the operators deciding whether to buy them. No vendor puff pieces, no filler. Just the stories worth your attention.

What we believe

Why OffPrem exists.

01

The industry deserved its own press

Restaurant tech has plenty of vendors telling their own story. It didn't have a press covering the whole industry — the deals, the economics, and the marketing behind them.

02

Operators are the audience, not an afterthought

Every story is written assuming the reader runs a restaurant or sells into one — not a general tech audience skimming for buzzwords.

03

Five minutes, not fifty

Coverage is built to be read in the time it takes to pour a coffee before the lunch rush — sharp, specific, and done.

Published by

Popcorn GTM.

Popcorn GTM is a marketing and go-to-market consultancy focused on restaurant technology, foodservice, and hospitality innovation. Founded by Paul Molinari, the firm helps emerging and growth-stage companies sharpen their positioning, clarify their message, and build marketing programs that support real commercial momentum — blending brand storytelling, category expertise, and sales-aligned execution drawn from agency, enterprise, and growth-stage environments, including nearly a decade helping scale CrunchTime into a category leader.

OffPrem is where that same category expertise turns into independent coverage: operator-literate, sales-aligned, and allergic to vendor spin. Editorial decisions on OffPrem are made independently of any Popcorn GTM client relationships.
